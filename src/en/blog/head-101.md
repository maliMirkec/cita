# Every great website starts with an organized head

Most people judge a website by what they see. However, design and aesthetics are only one side of a website. The technical part — the "hidden" side — is perhaps even more important. At the top of every HTML document lies the `<head>` section, which hides many challenges. When handled correctly, these challenges become opportunities to gain an edge over your competitors. At Studio CiTA, we've mastered the art of turning these technical hurdles into business opportunities.

## We aren't just talking `<head>`s

If you choose to use a website builder — AI-powered or otherwise — you likely won't even see the `<head>` section until it is too late. Most builders prioritize presenting ideas as quickly as possible. While speed is important, you must think long-term and organize your `<head>` code properly.

The `<title`> tag is what appears in your browser tab. Have you ever noticed how some websites change their title when the tab becomes inactive? We notice these details, and we ensure your visitors do too by implementing this feature for you.

Next to the title is the favicon. We ensure your favicon tags are in order and look crisp on every device, from high-resolution Retina displays to small mobile screens. Since favicons are much more than just a tiny icon, we provide a complete manifest of assets that make your site feel like a premium, native app.

Have you ever shared a link only to find it has no preview image on Slack, social networks, or chat apps? That is a missed opportunity we never let slip by. We ensure your Open Graph (OG) tags are correct and, as a bonus, we can integrate the automatic generation of perfect OG images. Your designer will be thrilled to hear about this feature.

By leveraging a correct `<head>` setup, you strengthen your brand on all fronts. Every visitor will see the same brand assets on every device, channel, or network, making your company more recognizable and memorable.

You can do even more by providing search engines with detailed information via Structured Data. We can define Rich Search Results snippets to help search engines display vital information — like your address, working hours, star reviews, and pricing — directly on the results page. By keeping your Knowledge Graph data up to date, you make it easier for customers to choose your business.

The `<head>` code isn't just for metadata. It is where page speed optimization begins. We know how to remove bottlenecks to ensure your website achieves optimal performance. We ensure all assets load in the correct order to eliminate render-blocking issues, so your content appears instantly.

Optimization is not a one-time job. We continuously monitor performance and make iterative improvements to ensure your website never drops its score on Core Web Vitals or other performance benchmarks.

Just to be clear, we ensure the technical integrity by default by implementing the standard engineering tags:

- Canonical Links: To ensure Google doesn't penalize you for duplicate content.
- Alternative Links: To ensure your content is consumed in the correct language.
- Referrer Policies: To protect your users' privacy and data security.
- Webmentions & Fediverse Tags: To place your business at the forefront of the modern, decentralized web.

Every business needs a website. It is the first thing a potential client will look at, and it deserves expert attention. Without an organized `<head>`, your site will struggle to become a high-performing business asset.
